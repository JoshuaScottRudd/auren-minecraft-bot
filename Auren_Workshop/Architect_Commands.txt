================================================================================
  CUTTING
================================================================================

  .\Cutting_room\cutting_room.ps1
  .\Cutting_room\cutting_room.ps1 --take=<stamp>

  Footage lands in:  footage\<date_time>\
  Cuts land in:      footage\edit\<project name>\

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  IN THE ROOM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Shelf        pick a take, pick let's play or highlight reel
  Let's play   one continuous window, camera changes, speed-ups
  Highlight    judge each clip in or out, trim, cut out, reorder
  Preview      drop music and voice on, mark where each line starts, print it

  Every surface saves as you work. Close the tab and open it again.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE LET'S PLAY KEYS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Space      play / pause
  ← →        5 seconds        Shift + ← →   30 seconds
  1 - 9      take that camera
  I  O       start / end of the video — trims the dead air at each end
  S          speed up a stretch — press at each end, Esc drops the mark
  E          teaser            T   grab the frame for a thumbnail

  Dead air before you start and after you stop: park on the real start,
  press I; park on the real end, press O. Everything outside is dropped.

  Inside that window nothing is removed. A slow stretch is sped up instead.
  1x puts a span back. A sped-up span is silent — game audio and your mic
  both drop. The music bed plays over it.

  What happened lists the WHOLE crew, including bots with no camera —
  their names are underlined. Clicking one jumps the timeline there.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  RENDER FROM THE TERMINAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  node Cutting_room/edit_renderer.js render --name=<project>
  node Cutting_room/edit_renderer.js render --name=<project> --final

  draft    540p, fast, no music
  final    1080p60, music and voice mixed
